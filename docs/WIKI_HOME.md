## Documentation rules

1. Distinguish **original OpenCode features**, **revival contributions**, and **DragonForge changes**.
2. Never label a proposed feature as shipped without testing it.
3. Preserve the original licence and attribution.
4. Preserve historical releases separately from DragonForge releases.
5. Do not overwrite working source, historical evidence, or upstream archives just to rebrand them.

These Markdown pages are wiki-ready source documents. They are not automatically published to the separate GitHub Wiki service.
